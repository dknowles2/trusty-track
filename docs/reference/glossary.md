# Glossary

The words Trusty Track uses, in alphabetical order.

**Auto-advance** — a toggle on the race screen. When on, the screen moves to
the next heat by itself ten seconds after results are recorded.

**Balanced round** — a round where the first heats are random and later
heats match cars that are doing about as well, so more children get a heat
they can win. See [Round styles](round-styles.md#balanced).

**Car** — the built-in word for what a racer's entry is called. A pack can
rename it — "Rocket" for a Space Derby, "Boat" for a Raingutter Regatta — and
pick which of three pictures goes with it; see
[the words on screen](race-settings.md#the-words-on-screen).

**Car number** — the number on the car, called out at the track and printed
on everything. How numbers are handed out is a race setting; see
[Race and track settings](race-settings.md#car-numbering).

**Championship round** — a round whose racers are picked from results rather
than from the roster: the fastest overall, the fastest from each den, the top
finishers of another round, or the slowest cars. See
[Championship rounds](championship-rounds.md).

**Check-in** — marking a car as inspected and cleared to race. Only
checked-in cars are put into heats.

**Check-in code** — a printed QR code, one per racer, that opens their
check-in form when scanned. See [Printed documents](printing.md#check-in-codes).

**Den** — a group within the race (Lions, Tigers, Wolves…). Used for
den-only rounds, per-den championships, car number ranges, and the den
tables on printed results. "Den" is the built-in word for this — a pack can
rename it, and anything else on screen, to fit a different kind of race; see
[the words on screen](race-settings.md#the-words-on-screen).

**Display** — any screen showing the Live page: a projector, a TV, a tablet
on the wall. Displays need no PIN and cannot change anything. See
[Audience display views](displays.md).

**Elimination round** — a round where a car is out after too many losses and
the last car left wins. See [Round styles](round-styles.md#elimination).

**Fake timer** — a pretend timer that makes up finishing times, for practice
and for trying the software out. Not for recording a real race with no
hardware — see [No timer](race-settings.md#no-timer) for that. See the
[Fake Timer guide](../fake-timer.md).

**Free race** — a heat that runs on the timer and shows on the audience
display but counts for nothing. See the [Free Race guide](../free-race.md).

**Heat** — one race down the track: a handful of cars, one per lane, ten to
thirty seconds.

**Heat sheet** — the schedule on paper, with an empty column to write
results into. See [Printed documents](printing.md#the-heat-sheet).

**Lane** — one slot on the track. Lanes are not equal — small differences in
the track make some faster — which is why the default round style gives every
car a turn in every lane.

**Line-up** — who is in a round's heats. A championship round's line-up
fills itself in from results; **Line-up out of date** on the schedule means
those results have since changed. See
[Championship rounds](championship-rounds.md#when-a-time-is-corrected).

**Master running order** — an opt-in race setting that interleaves every
den's heats into one running order instead of a block per den, so the track
need not idle while the next den's cars are staged. Off by default. See
[Running order across groups](running-order.md).

**Operator** — the person running the race, on the device that holds the
operator PIN (if one is set). See
[Roles and permissions](roles-and-permissions.md).

**Pit pass** — a lanyard-sized printed card with the racer's photo, name,
den, and car. See [Printed documents](printing.md#the-seven-documents).

**Points** — the scoring choice for racing without a timer: finishing places
added up, lowest total wins. See [No timer](race-settings.md#no-timer) for
setting a track up to race this way, and [Scoring](scoring.md#points) for the
rules.

**Qualifying round** — a round the whole roster races. The overall standings
are built from these, and championship rounds pick their racers from them.

**Round** — a set of heats. A qualifying round covers everyone; a
championship round covers the cars that qualified for it.

**Runs per lane** — how many times each car takes each lane in a round. One
is the default; more produces fairer averages and takes longer.

**Skipped heat** — a heat passed over without racing, usually because every
car in it scratched. See
[Changes in the middle of a race](mid-race-changes.md#skipping-a-heat).

**Standings** — the ranked results. The overall standings cover the
qualifying rounds; each championship round has its own page. See
[Scoring](scoring.md#what-the-overall-standings-cover).

**Terminology** — the words the app uses for a racing group, for the
organization itself, and for a racer's own entry — "Den", "Pack" and "Car"
by default, renameable install-wide or per race. See
[the words on screen](race-settings.md#the-words-on-screen).

**Timed** — the default scoring choice: each car's score is its average
time, fastest average wins. See [Scoring](scoring.md#timed).
